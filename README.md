A complete story in exactly six words: "Buried him Tuesday. He called Wednesday." — he was buried on Tuesday, yet he called on Wednesday. A whole hidden story in one line: a funeral, an impossible phone call, and everything the reader must fill in between the two days — mistaken identity, a faked death, or something that was never dead at all.
